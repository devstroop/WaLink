About WaLink

WaLink is a WhatsApp messaging platform designed for businesses, teams, and developers who need reliable, multi-account WhatsApp communication from a single place.

Whether you're a small business owner sending order updates, a support team managing customer conversations, or a developer integrating WhatsApp into your product — WaLink gives you the tools to do it efficiently.

What we offer

  • Multi-account management — Connect multiple WhatsApp numbers and manage them all from one dashboard.
  • Web-based messaging — Send and receive messages, share media, and manage groups right from your browser.
  • Automation — Integrate WhatsApp messaging into your workflows with webhooks and our API.
  • AI assistants — Let AI tools read and send messages on your behalf for smart auto-replies.
  • Team collaboration — Invite team members with role-based permissions to control access.
  • Real-time webhooks — Get notified instantly when messages arrive so you never miss a conversation.

Our mission

We believe WhatsApp communication should be simple to manage at any scale. WaLink removes the complexity so you can focus on what matters — connecting with your customers and growing your business.
